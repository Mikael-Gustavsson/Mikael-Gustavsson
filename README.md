# Hi, I'm Mikael

I'm a software engineer and research infrastructure architect based in
Umeå, Sweden. I have worked professionally with software since 2002,
across telecommunications, Swedish public services and research
infrastructure.

Today I work at Umeå University, where I combine hands-on engineering
with system architecture, technical coordination and configuration
management.

My current work includes:

- secure and scalable infrastructure for research data
- system architecture and integrations
- CI/CD and configuration management
- technical evaluation and procurement
- turning research and organisational needs into maintainable systems

## Background

I started my career developing and customer-adapting MMS software for
feature phones.

I later worked as a consultant in Swedish government projects creating
public-facing services for population registration, followed by several
years developing in-house CRM systems for a telecommunications provider.

Since 2018, my work has focused increasingly on research-related IT,
including data platforms, research archiving, digital consent and secure
infrastructure for sensitive research data.

## What I care about

I am interested in systems that must remain understandable, secure and
maintainable after the initial project is finished.

That includes architecture, automation, traceability, sensible
development workflows and the organisational structures surrounding
the technology.
