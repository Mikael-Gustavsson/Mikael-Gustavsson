# Hi, I'm Mikael

I'm a software engineer and research infrastructure architect based in
Umeå, Sweden.

I have been building software professionally since 2002, working across
telecommunications, Swedish public services, enterprise systems and
research infrastructure.

Today I work at Umeå University, where I combine hands-on engineering
with system architecture, technical coordination and configuration
management.

## What I work with

My current work includes:

- secure and scalable infrastructure for research data
- system architecture, integrations and central data services
- CI/CD, automation and configuration management
- technical evaluation and procurement
- translating research and organisational needs into maintainable systems

Much of my work sits at the intersection of software, infrastructure,
security, governance and long-term system ownership.

## Background

I started my career developing MMS software for feature phones and
adapting it for international mobile phone manufacturers and operators.

I later worked as a consultant on Swedish government projects, building
public-facing digital services related to the Swedish population
registration system.

After that, I spent several years developing in-house CRM systems for a
telecommunications provider.

Since 2018, my work has increasingly focused on research-related IT.
This has included research data platforms, metadata and archiving
solutions, digital consent management, system integrations and secure
infrastructure for sensitive research data.

Over time, my role has expanded from software development into technical
leadership, solution architecture, infrastructure and configuration
management. I still value hands-on engineering and staying close to how
systems are actually built, deployed and operated.

## What I care about

I care about systems that remain understandable, secure and maintainable
long after the initial project has finished.

That includes clear architecture, useful automation, traceability,
sensible development workflows and the organisational structures needed
to support the technology.

I am particularly interested in work where technical decisions must hold
up across development, operations, security, regulation and long-term
maintenance.
